See and hear Coach while you play
  1. PC game, one screen: click Overlay, then run the Coach PC overlay app (docs/pc-overlay.md) so arrows, tasks and memes sit on top of the game. Streaming? Add the Overlay link as an OBS Browser Source instead.
  2. PC VR (Quest Link, Air Link, Steam Link): same Overlay link pinned in the headset with XSOverlay, OVR Toolkit or Desktop+ (docs/vr.md). PC audio goes into the headset, so Coach is in your ear.
  3. Standalone Quest (casting): pair Bluetooth earbuds to the PC and wear one: teammates and the game in one ear, Coach in the other. The overlay stays on the PC screen; Meta does not allow third-party overlays inside Quest games.
  4. Windows: with "Open Coach windows" on, the Overlay and the chat open by themselves when you go live. Say "Coach, show overlay" to bring them back.
  5. Hands-free: press the headset button next to the mic and say "Coach, where do I go?". "Coach, stop talking" mutes, "Coach, talk to me" unmutes.

Pilot terms · Reset consent

Before Coach can see or hear your game

Coach is in a private pilot. While you are live it takes a picture of your shared screen every few seconds, and it records your voice only while you hold the mic. Both go to the Coach server and to Meta's AI service. Coach keeps them to get better. Read the pilot terms.
