Coach pilot program terms

Version pilot-2026-11-01. Effective November 1, 2026. Coach is run by Goals and Gambles LLC.

Coach is an AI sidekick that watches your game and talks to you while you play. It is in a private pilot. These terms say, in plain words, what Coach does with what it sees and hears, and what you agree to by using it.

The short version
  • While you are live, Coach takes a picture of your shared screen every few seconds.
  • When you hold the mic button, Coach records what you say until you let go.
  • Those pictures and recordings go to the Coach server and to Meta's AI service, which reads and transcribes them.
  • Coach keeps them, with its own replies and your ratings, to get better. You can ask us to delete yours.
  • Coach can be wrong. It is a helper, not a referee, and not a substitute for your own judgment.

1. What you let Coach see

When you click "Go live" and choose a window or screen to share, Coach captures a still image of it about every 4 seconds and sends each image to the Coach server. Coach also sends images to Meta's AI service so the model can describe what is happening. When you load a match video, the whole video is uploaded to the Coach server, and single frames from it are sent to Meta's AI service when Coach looks at a moment. Sharing stops the moment you click "Stop sharing" or close the browser tab.

2. What you let Coach hear

Coach only listens while you hold the mic button (or tap it to start and tap again to stop). The recording is sent to the Coach server and to Meta's AI service to be turned into text. Coach does not listen at any other time. Coach does not record game audio or voice chat with other players.

3. Where it goes and who provides the AI

The images and recordings are processed by Meta's AI service (the Meta Model API). Meta's own terms and privacy policy apply to what Meta does with them. Coach does not sell your images or recordings and does not share them with anyone else.

4. What Coach keeps

Coach stores the screen images it looked at, your transcribed words, its replies and callouts, your thumbs up or down, your corrections, and a short memory of each match. This is kept on the Coach server. We use it to find mistakes, improve Coach's instructions, and train the model that powers Coach. Your corrections are the most useful part of that. To have your data deleted, email us (see Contact) and we will remove it within 30 days.

5. What you agree to

  • You are 18 or older.
  • You will only share a screen, window or video that is yours to share. If other people appear or speak in it, you have their permission.
  • You will not share anything private, such as passwords, bank details or messages, while Coach is watching. Pick the game window, not your whole desktop, where you can.
  • Some games forbid overlays, screen capture or outside help in their rules. Checking a game's rules before using Coach with it is your responsibility.
  • You will not use Coach to cheat in ranked or competitive play where the game's rules forbid it.

6. Pilot program, no promises

Coach is unfinished software in a pilot. It can be wrong, late or silent. It can stop working, change or end at any time without notice. It is provided as is, with no warranty of any kind, and Goals and Gambles LLC is not responsible for anything that happens in a game or elsewhere because you followed, or did not follow, what Coach said. Use at your own risk.

7. Stopping

You can stop at any time: click "Stop sharing", let go of the mic, or close the tab. To withdraw your consent fully, click "Reset consent" at the bottom of the Coach page; Coach will then ask you to agree again before it sees or hears anything. Withdrawing consent does not delete what was already stored; ask us by email for that.

8. Changes

If these terms change in a way that matters, Coach will show the new version and ask you to agree again before it sees or hears anything.
